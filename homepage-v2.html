Who we are

Two lines of business, one owner group.

Elite Real Estate Group is a privately owned commercial real estate company in Edmonton. Elite is in the business of acquiring assets such as retail shopping centres, apartment complexes and industrial buildings. The second arm of the company is actively involved in the development of retail shopping centres and commercial buildings.

The ownership is experienced in creating and enhancing the value of their assets, and the portfolio includes several international, national and regional tenants.

Dedicated to enhancing the value of its assets and driving growth in the commercial real estate market.
